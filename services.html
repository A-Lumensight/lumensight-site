What We Do

Four disciplines. One integrated practice.

Lumensight delivers technology and AI strategy, engineering, security, and cloud transformation to organizations in regulated industries — where the margin for error is thin and the compliance bar is high.

Service 01

Technology Strategy & Enterprise Architecture

Technology investments without strategic alignment become expensive experiments. We work with leadership teams to build enterprise architectures, capability models, AI adoption roadmaps, and governance frameworks that connect every initiative to measurable business outcomes. In regulated sectors, that alignment isn't a nice-to-have — it's what auditors and boards expect to see.

Service 02

Software Engineering & DevOps

We design and build the software and AI-powered systems regulated organizations depend on, engineered for auditability and long-term maintainability — not just working code. Our DevOps practice brings CI/CD automation, infrastructure as code, and disciplined testing to teams that need to move faster without compromising compliance. The result is delivery velocity that holds up under scrutiny.

Service 03

Cybersecurity

A security failure in critical infrastructure, healthcare data, or government systems carries consequences that go well beyond a bad headline. We build security programs — spanning risk assessment, threat modeling, AI governance, and incident response — that are proportionate to real threats and defensible under audit. Compliance follows from good security in our approach, not the other way around.

Service 04

Cloud Adoption & Digital Transformation

Cloud migration in a regulated environment means data residency, encryption key management, and network segmentation decisions your auditors can trace end to end. We guide organizations through cloud strategy and migration with governance built in from the start, not retrofitted after launch. The outcome is a modern operating model your compliance team can actually sign off on.
